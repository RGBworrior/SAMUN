THE PRINCIPAL'S DESK

A Message from our Principal

"The future belongs to those who dare to imagine a better world and have the courage to build it."

At Salwan Public School, Mayur Vihar, we believe that the purpose of education is not merely to prepare young people for examinations, but to prepare them to understand the world and their place within it. Our guiding vision, तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय, reminds us of the journey from ignorance to understanding, from uncertainty to wisdom, and from knowledge to purposeful action. The tenth edition of Salwan Model United Nations (SAMUN) embodies this spirit of learning. It brings the world into the classroom and transforms students from passive learners into active participants in conversations that shape our collective future. Through debate, diplomacy and deliberation, delegates encounter perspectives that may differ from their own and learn that every global challenge carries multiple dimensions. MUN is, at its heart, an exercise in understanding before responding. It teaches students to research before they speak, to listen before they judge, and to negotiate before they conclude. Whether discussing questions of peace and security, human rights, development, climate, or international cooperation, delegates are encouraged to move beyond simply identifying problems and begin thinking about meaningful and sustainable solutions.

SAMUN therefore offers much more than an opportunity to represent a country. It offers students an opportunity to step into the shoes of decision-makers, appreciate the complexity of global affairs, and recognise the responsibility that comes with having a voice. In doing so, it nurtures qualities that every future leader and responsible citizen must possess empathy, critical thinking, courage and integrity.

As we gather for the tenth edition of SAMUN, I encourage every delegate to embrace the experience wholeheartedly. Question deeply. Listen generously. Debate fearlessly. Collaborate sincerely. Let your preparation give strength to your arguments and your understanding give depth to your words. May SAMUN leave you with more than awards and memories. May it leave you with new questions, wider perspectives and a stronger belief in the possibility of collective action.

Capt. Richa Sharma Katyal Principal Salwan Public School, Mayur Vihar
A MESSAGE FROM THE SECRETARIAT

Welcome to SAMUN

Honneur en vérité

Greetings Delegates!

With the belief that every meaningful conversation begins with the courage to speak and the willingness to listen, the Secretariat of Salwan Model United Nations warmly welcomes you to the 10th edition of SAMUN. Over the years, SAMUN has grown into a platform where young minds come together to engage with the world beyond their classrooms. It is a space to question ideas, understand different perspectives, and engage in discussions that extend far beyond the confines of a committee room.

As we mark a decade of SAMUN, this edition brings together a new generation of voices, each with its own perspective and approach to the issues before them. We look forward to seeing these voices come together through thoughtful debate, lively discussion, and the negotiations that make every committee unique.

We look forward to welcoming you to the floor.

— The SAMUN Secretariat
