OUR PURPOSE

A Platform for Ideas.

SAMUN is more than a platform for debate. It is where young minds engage with the world’s most pressing challenges, challenge perspectives, and learn the art of diplomacy. Here, every speech carries a purpose, every negotiation shapes an outcome, and every resolution reflects the power of collective thought. We believe the leaders of tomorrow are built through the conversations we have today.

Every speech matters. Every alliance has a purpose. Every resolution is an opportunity to make an impact. We Welcome the next generation of global diplomats to the 10th Edition of Salwan Model United Nations, hosted at Salwan Public School, Mayur Vihar. Join two days of rigorous academic debate, strategic lobbying, and collaborative resolution drafting addressing the frontier of modern geopolitics.
